RESEARCH ENTRY

Ways into real research

MESA CENTERLAVC's MESA Center (LARC 229) supports STEM students with programs, community, and academic resources.Also home base of PESSA, the pre-med and science scholars club; joining both is encouraged.
SUMMER PROGRAMSResearch programs that accept community-college students, including UC-hosted summer research opportunities.Deadlines cluster in winter and early spring; the club workshops applications at research-entry night.
VOLUNTEER ROLESClinical and research volunteer positions at LA-area institutions.Members share leads from their own workplaces and applications; officers verify each posting before it goes on the list.
TRANSFER NAVIGATION

The path to a four-year program

UCLA CaSBComputational & Systems Biology at UCLA College, the flagship local comp-bio destination.We map its preparation coursework onto the LAVC catalog together.
UC BIOINFORMATICSBioinformatics and computational-biology majors across UC campuses.Requirements differ by campus; the club keeps a comparison sheet current.
ASSIST.ORGThe official course-articulation system between California community colleges and UC/CSU.Every plan starts here; we teach new members to read it at transfer-pathway night.
LAVC TRANSFER CENTERCounselor-verified transfer planning on campus.The club's plans are peer navigation, never a substitute for a counselor's sign-off; we go together.
