PROJECTS

One project a semester. Everyone builds.

The club's center of gravity is a single communal project, chosen by member vote at the kickoff meeting, scoped so that every skill level has real work: data wrangling, annotation, analysis, visualization, writing. No coding experience required to contribute; plenty of room to learn it along the way.

FALL 2026 BALLOT

Candidate directions

The founding cohort votes at the kickoff meeting. Members can also propose their own direction, and the strongest proposal wins regardless of this list.

CANDIDATE A

Open-dataset bioinformatics build

Work a public genomic or protein dataset into a documented group analysis: sequence puzzles first, a real pipeline by December. Beginner-friendly on-ramp through structured problem sets.

CANDIDATE B

Medical-imaging analysis

Explore a public retinal or medical-imaging dataset as a group: what the images show, how they become measurements, and what simple computation can pull out of them.

CANDIDATE C

Member proposal

Bring the idea you have been carrying around. If the room votes for it, the club builds it. Proposals take five minutes at kickoff: what, why, and what a beginner could contribute.

HOW IT RUNS

Project rules

  • Every skill level contributes. Tasks are scoped in tiers, from labeling and documentation to analysis code.
  • Everything documented. The build is open to all members; the semester ends with a visible artifact each contributor can point to.
  • Project nights carry it. First and third Tuesday, 6:30–7:30 PM, hybrid; small groups advance the build between meetings at their own pace.
