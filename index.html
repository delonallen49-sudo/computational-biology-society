WHAT WE DO

One club, three engines

COMMUNAL PROJECTS

Build one thing, together

Each semester the club votes on one communal project, an open-dataset bioinformatics build or a medical-imaging analysis, structured so every member contributes at their own skill level, with visible artifacts by semester's end.

RESEARCH ENTRY

Get into real research

Getting into research alone is the hard way. We pool entry points (faculty projects, campus programs, volunteer roles) and prep each other's applications as a group.

TRANSFER NAVIGATION

Map the path out

UCLA Computational & Systems Biology, UC bioinformatics majors, articulation on ASSIST: we chart the coursework and deadlines together.
